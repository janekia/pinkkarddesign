Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

About This Policy

This policy explains how PinkkardDesign LLC handles information associated with pinkkarddesign.com and website inquiries.

Information You Provide

When you use the contact form, you may provide your name, email address, business or organization name, current website address, service selection, and project message. We also receive information you choose to share by email.

Please do not send passwords, payment card details, or sensitive personal information through the contact form.

How Information Is Used

  • Responding to inquiries and discussing projects
  • Preparing quotes and coordinating requested services
  • Keeping records of project communications
  • Maintaining website security and reducing spam
  • Understanding website traffic and improving the site

Contact Form and Hosting

The website is hosted through Netlify, which also processes contact form submissions. Hosting and form providers may process submitted information and technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to operate and secure their services.

For more information, see Netlify’s Privacy Statement .

Google Analytics and Cookies

PinkkardDesign uses Google Analytics to understand website visits, traffic sources, and how visitors interact with pages.

Depending on the browser, consent choices, and Analytics settings, this may involve cookies or similar technologies and information such as pages viewed, browser and device details, approximate location, and referral sources.

PinkkardDesign currently uses Analytics for traffic measurement, rather than advertising or remarketing.

You can manage or block cookies in your browser. Google also offers a Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on for supported browsers. Blocking cookies may affect some website functions.

See Google’s Privacy Policy for information about Google’s handling of data.

Google Search Console

PinkkardDesign uses Google Search Console to monitor how this website performs in Google Search, including search impressions, clicks, search queries, and page performance.

Search Console reports help improve the website’s search visibility; it is separate from the contact form.

Other Website Resources

The website loads resources such as fonts, icons, and scripts from third-party providers, including Google Fonts and jsDelivr.

Your browser may send technical information such as your IP address and browser details to those providers when requesting these resources.

Sharing and Service Providers

Information may be processed by providers used for hosting, form handling, email, and analytics as needed to operate the website and respond to requests.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect rights and website security.

Retention and Security

Inquiry and project information is retained as needed to respond, provide requested services, maintain business records, and meet applicable obligations.

Analytics retention depends on the configured service settings. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your Choices and Requests

You may email info@pinkkarddesign.com to ask about information you submitted or request correction or deletion.

Requests will be handled subject to applicable law and any records that must be retained. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings.

External Links

Links to client websites, social media, and other services lead to websites with their own privacy policies. Review those policies when visiting those services.

Policy Updates and Contact

This policy may be updated when website practices change. The updated date will appear on this page.

For privacy questions, contact info@pinkkarddesign.com .
